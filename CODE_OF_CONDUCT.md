# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version
2.1, available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: be respectful, welcoming and constructive; harassment and personal attacks are not
tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **info@pantechdynamics.com** with "Code of conduct" in the subject. All reports are reviewed
promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and
temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behavior they deem inappropriate, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
